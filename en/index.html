FUSETA

FUSETA is an app where a pixel-art egg grows while your phone lies face down. The friend that hatches lives in a tiny room, and you can trade the acorns it finds for furniture.

How to use

  1. Keep FUSETA open and place your phone face down
  2. Time keeps counting even if the screen turns off
  3. Pick your phone up to see how long it was face down, and your friend grows

Time spent in other apps does not count.

FAQ

The time is not counted

Place your phone face down while FUSETA is on screen. If you open another app first, the time is not counted.

A very long time was recorded

If a record is longer than 3 hours, the app asks whether to keep it when you pick up your phone. If you were using your phone during that time, choose "Discard".

The Friends Pack is gone after I changed phones

Tap "Restore Purchases" on the Friends Pack screen. If you bought it with the same Apple Account, you will not be charged again. Your friends' growth and your records are stored only on your device, so they cannot be moved to a new device.

I can't hear the music

The music does not play when the silent switch on your iPhone is on. You can also turn it on or off with "♪ On / Off" above the room.
