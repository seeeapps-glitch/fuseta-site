FUSETA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This policy explains how Seee (the "Developer") handles information in the smartphone app "FUSETA" (the "App"). This English version is a translation; if it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information the App handles

Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Developer's servers.

  • When your phone was face down, and those records
  • Your characters' growth and which character you chose
  • Your acorns and the furniture in your room
  • Settings (such as music on or off)

If you delete the App, this information is deleted too.

Motion sensor

The App uses your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) to tell whether the phone is lying face down. The sensor values are used only for this check on your device and are never stored or sent.

Purchase information

The App uses RevenueCat, Inc. to handle the purchase of the Friends Pack (a one-time in-app purchase) and to check whether you have bought it. When you buy it or when the App checks your purchase, your App Store purchase history and an anonymous user ID created by the App for each device are sent to RevenueCat. Neither the Developer nor RevenueCat receives your name, email address, or credit card number. Payment is handled by Apple.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

2. Purpose of use

The information the Developer handles is used only to provide the App's features, to handle and check the Friends Pack purchase, and to answer your questions.

3. Sharing with third parties

Except where required by law, your information is not shared with third parties (other than the sending to RevenueCat described in section 1).

4. Analytics and ads

The App does not use analytics tools or ads.

5. Children

The App can be used by people of any age. The App never sends personal information to the Developer, and the Developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Buying the Friends Pack follows the App Store's age and parental settings.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the Developer will announce it in the App or on the Developer's website.
